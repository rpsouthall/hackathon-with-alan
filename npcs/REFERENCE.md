# Art direction reference

User supplied a Kyoto environment screenshot during creation. This is a palette and world-style reference, not a request to reproduce its architecture or camera.

Observed: matte blue-grey roofs, dark warm timber, cream plaster, muted coral bridges, sage landscaping and pale pink cherry blossoms. Shapes are readable and stylized, lit softly without metallic shine or outline effects. The environment has a comfortable miniature/diorama quality.

Character interpretation: retain the requested voxel grid and articulated cuboid forms; use desaturated blue work jackets, warm cream collars, ochre accessories, coral café clothing and sage/indigo inn clothing. Slightly enlarged heads and small graphic faces make characters readable in third person. Outfit hems, pockets and back details provide identity from behind. Avoid saturated neon, weapons, gritty surface noise and high-frequency texture patterns.

Compare in three-quarter, front, back and side views, then verify against the actual exported environment as a local optional preview. Camera-match requirements for reproducing an attached object do not apply because the supplied image is of the environment, not a character model.
