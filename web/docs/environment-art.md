### cafe

Asset: `public/environments/kyoto-cafe-v1.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: widescreen 16:9 background for a Japanese language-learning game's conversation screen. Primary request: a welcoming Kyoto coffee shop interior seen from the customer's eye level, facing a clear wooden service counter. Style: polished cinematic game environment illustration, softly realistic 3D materials with hand-painted warmth, coherent perspective, natural architectural scale. Scene: dark walnut and honey-toned timber, ceramic coffee cups and a small espresso machine arranged at the edges, cream plaster walls, warm hanging paper lamps, a large window at the left looking onto a quiet Kyoto lane and green plants, an unoccupied counter through the lower foreground. Composition: wide eye-level image; leave the central area behind the counter calm and unobstructed to accommodate a separately overlaid live video avatar; architectural details live toward edges, quieter darker detail on the right for a dialogue panel. Lighting: late afternoon sunlight, amber pools of light, gentle sage-green accents, inviting and serene. Constraints: environment only, no people, no human figures, no avatar, no readable text, no menus with lettering, no logos, no interface, no watermarks, no baked-in vignette; make the entire image a single finished scene.
